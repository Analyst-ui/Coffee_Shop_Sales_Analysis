# Coffee_Shop_Sales_Analysis


## Coffee Sales Analysis Dashboard using Microsoft Excel

An interactive Excel dashboard that analyzes coffee sales performance, customer behavior, and product trends to help business stakeholders make data-driven decisions, that will enhance the performance of a Coffee Shop.


## Project Overview

This project analyzes coffee sales data using Microsoft Excel. The objective is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and an interactive dashboard.
The dashboard enables users to explore sales trends, identify top-performing products, understand customer purchasing behavior, and monitor regional performance.


## Problem Statement

Coffee businesses often collect large amounts of sales data but struggle to extract actionable insights.
This project answers questions such as:

How do sales vary by vary by day of the week and hour of the day?

Are there any peak times for sales activity?

What is the total sales revenue for each month?

How do sales vary across different store locations?

What is the average price/order per person?

Which products are the best-selling in terms of quantity and revenue?

How do sales vary by product category?


## Dataset

The dataset contains coffee sales transactions as Coffee_Shop_Sales_Data.xlsx

## Key Insights & Recommendations

##### Business Insights:

-Sales are highest on weekdays. Peak sales occur during morning (7 AM–10 AM).

-The busiest sales periods occur during the morning commute. Customer traffic is significantly lower during late evening hours.

-Monthly revenue shows clear fluctuations, with the highest sales recorded in peak months. Some months experience lower sales, indicating seasonal demand patterns.

-Certain store locations consistently generate higher revenue than others. Lower-performing locations may experience reduced customer traffic or different purchasing patterns.

-A small number of products contribute a significant share of total sales. Coffee and tea products generate higher sales & revenue.

-Certain product categories like coffee and tea outperform others in both sales volume and revenue. Some other categories have lower demand and may require promotional support.

##### Business Recommendations:

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

-Increase staffing during peak sales hours to reduce customer wait times.

-Ensure adequate stock for top-selling products to prevent shortages.

-Launch targeted promotions in low-performing stores and months.

-Strengthen customer loyalty programs to encourage repeat purchases.

-Use bundle offers and cross-selling to increase average order value.

-Forecast inventory using historical monthly sales trends.

-Focus marketing efforts on high-demand product categories while improving visibility of lower-performing ones.
